## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No. Credits never expire. Use them at your own pace.

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes — 2 free credits at signup, no credit card required.

**What's the difference between free and paid credits?**
Free credits include basic analysis (score, summary, pain points). Paid credits unlock full analysis: SEO keywords, product ideas, acquisition channels, AI prompts, and export.

**Can I buy more credits later?**
Yes. Any pack can be purchased at any time to top up.
